Origami Joy makes paper folding clear, calm and fun. Pick a model, follow one clear step at a time, and when a fold gets tricky, replay that exact step as a short video clip.

HOW IT HELPS YOU FOLD
• One clear step at a time – big step numbers, clean diagrams, and a thumbnail strip to jump to any step. Leave a model and pick up right where you stopped.
• Video clips for the tricky bits – many models include a clip cut to the exact step you are on, so you can watch the hands fold it and replay it as often as you like.
• Origami, 3D origami and kirigami – more than 140 models: animals, flowers, boxes, toys, geometric shapes and paper-cut snowflakes. Every model shows its difficulty, number of steps and estimated time, and every model is open to fold – nothing is locked.
• Easy to explore – browse collections, search by name, and filter or sort by category, difficulty and time. Heart your favourites to find them again.

PLAY MINI FOLD
Take a break with Mini Fold, a relaxing paper-folding puzzle. Swipe or tap to fold the sheet, reveal hidden pictures level by level, earn coins and bloom new paper themes. Coins are earned by playing – they cannot be bought with real money – and a daily bonus keeps them coming.

SNAP YOUR FINISHED MODEL
When you finish a fold, take a photo of your model with the camera or pick one from your gallery. Your photos live in Creations on your device, ready to look back on. The camera is optional – the rest of the app works without it.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Light and dark appearance, plus adjustable text size.
• 12 languages: English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Vietnamese and Chinese.
• Optional daily folding reminder – turn it on or off in Profile.
• No account or sign-in needed. Open the app and start folding.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Origami Joy contains ads.
• Models, diagrams and clips are loaded from our servers, so you need an Internet connection to browse and fold models.
• The app asks for Camera access only if you choose to take a photo of your finished model, and for notification permission only if you want the daily reminder.
• We use analytics and crash reports to improve the app. See our Privacy Policy: https://build-data-solutions-sas.github.io/origami-joy/privacy/
