FAQ
Does every model have a video?

No. Every model has step-by-step diagrams; many models also have video clips, and those are marked in the app.

Is Origami Joy free?

It is free to download and supported by ads. Every model is open — nothing is locked — and you don't need an account.

Do I need an Internet connection?

Yes, to browse and fold models: guides, images and clips are loaded from our servers.

Do I have to use the camera?

No. The camera is only used if you choose to take a photo of your finished model, and you can pick a photo from your gallery instead. Photos stay on your device.

Can I buy coins?

No. Coins in Mini Fold are earned by playing and through the daily bonus; they are not sold for real money.

Which languages are supported?

English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Vietnamese and Chinese.
